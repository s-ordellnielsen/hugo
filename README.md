#  Hugo

Hugo is an app that helps individuals that are Jehovahs Witnesses keep track of their reports, whether they are regular pioneers, auxiliary pioneers or participating as auxiliary pioneers in a campaign. The app should help them keep track of their goals (yearly or monthly). Every feature this app adds should be in the spirit of making it easier and simpler to keep track of this.

## Features
- **Add entries.** Add entries to the report.
- **Send report to group overseer.** At the end of each month, the app should be able to send an SMS to the users group overseer at the tap of a button. (The message itself is customizable)
- **Plan.** Schedule your field service and plan out the month.
- **Track.** Keep track of your goal, see if you're ahead or behind.

## Screens
- **Overview:** Focus on the current month, see how many hours you've spent in the field this current month and see if you're ahead or behind your schedule and goal.
- **Plan:** Plan upcoming months and see how your actual field service stacks up to your plan.
- **Year:** See how the current theocratic year looks. See your report for each month of the year and if you have a yearly goal sum it together and see how close you are to reaching your goal, also include how far the publisher "should" be at this point to reach the yearly goal at the end of the theocratic year.

## Principles
- **Simplicity.** Always keep the app as simple as possible, do not add unnecessary features. No fluff that is unrelated should be added.
- **Ease of use.** The app should be accessible to all, the user base ranges from around 13 to 70 years. As there will be a lot of older users, the app should always explain itself and make good use of accessibility features. Meanwhile the app should also cater to the younger audience.
- **Modularity.** The app will be used by non-tech users and power users. So the app should be modular and be able to adapt to the users needs. But this should under no circumstance be at the expense of the **Simplicity** and **Ease of use** principles.
