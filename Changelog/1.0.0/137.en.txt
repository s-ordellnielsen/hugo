Hugo is back with a new update. School hours, a new welcome, and a clearer view of your yearly goal. Let's dive in.

WELCOME
- A new welcome shows what Hugo does, followed by short setup pages for your group overseer and your settings.
- Type your group overseer's name and number yourself instead of choosing a contact.
- The setup always shows all four steps, and the privacy and iCloud notes are easier to spot.

CATEGORIES
- Every category is now either Field Service or Credit. Two cards explain the difference when you create a category.
- You can only add Bible studies to entries in Field Service categories.
- The category list closes when you tap a category.

YEAR
- Your yearly goal is now a bar, with a marker for where you should be today.
- Add school hours, such as Pioneer School, to a month from the Year tab. They count toward your yearly goal and are sent with your report.
- Tap a month's card to open its details.
- Your target for the current month now grows a little each day.

HELP
- Help is now a menu in the toolbar, shown as a question mark.
- Two new articles explain how Hugo works out your target this month, and the difference between Field Service and Credit.

SETTINGS
- Settings now has a header at the top.
- iCloud shows whether sync is working, and says Offline when your device has no network.
- Your publisher status now syncs between your devices.
- The Monthly Target screen is clearer.
- On the Buffer page, choose where the Buffer counts: in Overview, in the Year tab, or both. Planner always includes it.
- Write a Review and Recommend Hugo are now in Settings.

CHANGES
- Titles on content screens, tab roots and sheets use the serif font
- The submit button on the month page uses your accent color
- The progress bars in Planner and Year have a white track
- Fixed a freeze when opening the Buffer page
- Fixed missing text on the group overseer step
- General bug fixes and improvements
