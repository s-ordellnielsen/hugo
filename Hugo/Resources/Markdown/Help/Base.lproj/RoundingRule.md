# Rounding

Reports are always submitted in whole hours. If your monthly total time is not a whole number of hours, the rounding rule determines what happens to the remaining minutes.

## Important

Always enter and report your actual time in Hugo. Do **not** add or subtract minutes from the total before sending your report.

Instead, select the rounding rule that applies to you and let Hugo calculate the submitted hours. Hugo keeps track of the actual time, the rounding adjustments, and any minutes transferred to the following month.

If you adjust the total yourself, Hugo cannot distinguish those minutes from your actual time. This can cause your yearly totals to become inaccurate.

## What this means for you

- You do not need to calculate or adjust the total manually before submitting your report to your group overseer.
- The rounding rule is applied automatically when Hugo prepares your report.
- Your actual recorded time is kept separate from the rounded hours that are submitted.
- Hugo can use this information to calculate more accurate yearly totals.

Changing the rounding rule does not change your recorded entries. It only changes how the current report is calculated.

## Round up

The remaining minutes count as one additional whole hour.

For example, **1 hour and 30 minutes** is submitted as **2 hours**.

## Round down

The remaining minutes are removed from the submitted total.

For example, **1 hour and 30 minutes** is submitted as **1 hour**.

## Transfer to next month

The remaining minutes are saved and added to the following month's time.

For example, **1 hour and 30 minutes** is submitted as **1 hour**, but the remaining 30 minutes will be added to the next month.

## Which rule should I choose?

Choose the rule that fits your needs the best, only you know how you report your time.

You can select your default rule in settings and can be customized on a per report basis when submitting a report.
