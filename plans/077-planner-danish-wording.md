## Why this matters

The owner reviewed the Danish text that the Planner build adds, and decided on three changes before the build goes to testers:

1. **The Overview plan status says the opposite of what it means.** "Behind Schedule" is translated as "Efter plan", but in Danish "efter planen" usually means "according to plan". A tester who is behind reads that they are on track. The owner chose "Bagud" / "Langt bagud", which also matches the Year page header ("3 timer bagud"). The "ahead" labels get the definite form "planen".
2. **"Måltal" becomes "Mål".** The Planner calls the monthly target "måltal" in nine strings. The owner finds "mål" more natural Danish. Because "mål" also means the yearly goal elsewhere, the one Planner line that talks about the yearly goal says "årsmål" instead, so the two meanings never sit side by side as the same word.
3. **"Stadig planlagt" becomes "Tilbage i planen"**, which reads more naturally in the Planner header ("Udført 20 timer · Tilbage i planen 10 timer").
